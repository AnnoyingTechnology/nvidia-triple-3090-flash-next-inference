# Benchmarks and quality

All cells: 225 W per card, low reasoning, T 1 / top-p 0.95 / top-k 20 / min-p 0, seed 42.
Performance cells stop at 512 tokens and carry no quality score. Power is the median of 0.5 s
board-power samples summed over all three cards (including the idle vision/desktop card).
Summary with every request hash: [placement-20261004-summary.json](../results/public/placement-20261004-summary.json).

## Matched decode and prefill probe

`scripts/context_decode_probe.py` sends one document plus one of three questions per cell. The
32K/64K/128K documents (generated by `fixtures.py` from the pinned Strata source and tokenizer) and
all 32K/128K request hashes are identical to the Ulmus ABBA cells. Order 32, 128, 128, 32, 32, 128:
the first read of each size is cold; one-slot history means a size change re-reads most of the prompt.

| Placement, launch | 32K decode | 128K decode | 32K first read | 128K first read | Power 32K / 128K |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| One card GPU1, a | 72.6, 74.7, 71.8 | 68.7, 65.6, 63.4 | 31,803 in 14.94 s | 130,107 in 63.87 s | 241 / 240 W |
| **Split GPU1+GPU2, a** | **93.7, 93.1, 94.1** | **86.0, 84.0, 82.2** | 31,803 in 12.36 s | 113,723 in 35.54 s | 363 / 432 W |
| **Split GPU1+GPU2, c** | **91.0, 96.7, 95.4** | **82.7, 83.2, 81.0** | 31,803 in 12.58 s | 113,723 in 36.22 s | 372 / 436 W |
| Three stages GPU1, GPU0, GPU2, a | 93.1, 95.4, 92.3 | 81.2, 92.1, 86.6 | 31,803 in 15.45 s | 113,723 in 44.53 s | 457 / 485 W |
| Ulmus selected24 (published) | median 120.10 | median 112.55 | 31,803 in 6.81 s | 113,723 in 24.58 s | ~264 W |

MTP acceptance per drafted token is 0.70-0.81, similar to Ulmus. The decode expert-cache hit rate
on the split is 97.6-98.5%. The selected split was relaunched as `qwen3.8-flash-next.service` and
reproduced 2,563 tok/s prefill and 96.5 tok/s decode on the cold 32K cell.

## Quality

Practical30 v2 (`practical_eval.py`, deck SHA-256 `03b7d02ea58fef79b8bcc28a94b5c43a9735168145cd0f50a39f0bb0b44ed0ba`,
identical to Ulmus): **29/30** at low, seed 42, all 30 stopped naturally under a 32,768-token runaway
guard; median completed-answer time 9.04 s, longest 46.8 s, median 940 completion tokens. The failure
is `path-route`. Every function's positive and negative controls pass in the isolated grader
(`--network none`, read-only, no capabilities, 512 MiB, 1 CPU, 64 PIDs). Ulmus selected scored
29/30 failing `literal-endpoint`; original IQ3 scored 28/29/30 across three seeds, so single-case
differences are not resolvable. [Results](../results/public/practical30-gpu12-low-s42.json).

Vision15 (`vision_compare.py`, tracked deck): **14/15** content and **14/15** strict JSON, all natural,
no transport errors. The failure is `invoice` (`total_due` returned as the string `"480.00"`), the same
case as Ulmus. First token for a new image: median 2.18 s, range 0.98-2.53 s (Ulmus median 1.68 s).
[Results](../results/public/vision15-gpu12-low-20261004.json).

Maximum-budget 2048x2048 images (`scripts/check_resident_large_images.py`, the Ulmus payloads):
3/3 natural `READY`, no LEND/RECLAIM, cached repeat reusing 4,142 tokens. New-image first token 7.1 s
versus 3.7 s on Ulmus: about 4.2 s in the encoder on GPU0 (100% SM at its 225 W cap) plus 2.9 s of
engine prompt reading. [Results](../results/public/large-images-gpu12-b-20261004.json).

API contract (`owner_api_check.py`): 9/9 on launches a, b, c and the service launch: model/context/vision
status, sampling and output defaults, low default, implicit low and explicit `none` requests, validated
JSON schema over SSE, invalid schema and format-plus-tools rejection, explicit output cap.

## Component parity

[native-components.json](../results/public/native-components.json): 8/8 on GPU1 with the real IQ3_S
shard. Native expert parity: AVX2 IQ3_XXS/IQ4_XS gate/up and Q2_0/IQ4_NL down kernels against ggml
(relative error ~3e-8 to 2e-4), GPU dequantization bit-identical to ggml, CPU-GPU relative difference
~1.6e-2 (normal quantized arithmetic), zero failures.

## Limitations

Single seed per quality deck; local canaries, not blind public benchmarks; no full-size parity claim.
Board power only, no wall-plug energy. Launch-to-launch decode spread is about +/-2%.
